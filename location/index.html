How the master switch works

ON: Cloudflare accepts new OwnTracks positions and authorized Location members can view new positions. OFF: the updated Worker stops storing new positions and the website stops sharing them. Previously saved trip history remains intact.

OwnTracks may still collect positions on your iPhone in Move mode. The master switch controls Cloudflare recording, not iPhone location services. A position sent before the latest ON time is ignored.
