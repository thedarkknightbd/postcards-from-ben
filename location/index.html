Tracking versus sharing

Sharing determines whether the site can provide location information to approved members. Tracking is OwnTracks sending positions to the separate Worker. This website control does not stop OwnTracks uploads or clear the D1 location history.

Live coordinates are shown only to approved Location members while sharing is enabled and the protected Worker connection has been verified. Previous public location pages remain retired.
