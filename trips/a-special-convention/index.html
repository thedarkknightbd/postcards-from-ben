A Special Convention

A journey through Romania and Greece centered around a very special gathering.

The Journey

From Bucharest to Mykonos

This journey began in Bucharest before continuing to Cluj for the special convention. I stayed in Cluj through August 23, then flew to Athens and finished the trip with two days on Mykonos. It became a trip that mixed a very meaningful gathering with some unforgettable time in Romania and Greece.
